INTRODUCTION

Welcome to Nova Software Inc. This handbook describes the policies, benefits, and expectations
that apply to all full-time employees. Part-time employees and contractors should refer to their
specific agreements for differences. Nova Software reserves the right to update these policies
at any time with reasonable notice to employees.

Nova Software was founded in 2014 with the belief that great software comes from great people.
Our mission is to build tools that help distributed teams work together more effectively. As we
grow, maintaining a strong culture of transparency, ownership, and mutual respect is essential.
This handbook reflects our commitment to creating a workplace where everyone can do their best work.

Nothing in this handbook constitutes a contract of employment. Employment at Nova Software is
at-will unless a separate written agreement states otherwise.

SECTION 1: WORK HOURS AND ATTENDANCE

1.1 Standard Work Hours

Nova Software operates on a flexible schedule. Employees are expected to be available during
core hours from 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in their local time zone, Monday through Friday. Outside
of core hours, employees may arrange their eight-hour workday as needed, provided they meet
their responsibilities and attend required meetings. The company does not track time by the
minute; what matters is output and availability during core hours.

1.2 Overtime

Non-exempt employees must obtain manager approval before working more than 40 hours per week.
Approved overtime is compensated at 1.5 times the standard hourly rate. Exempt employees are
not eligible for overtime compensation but are expected to manage their workload within a
sustainable schedule. If a manager becomes aware that an exempt employee is regularly working
excessive hours, they should immediately discuss workload and priorities with the employee.

1.3 Attendance and Punctuality

Employees are expected to notify their manager at least one hour before the start of their
workday if they will be late or absent. Repeated unexcused absences may result in disciplinary
action up to and including termination. A pattern of late arrivals during core hours will be
addressed through the standard performance review process.

SECTION 2: PAID TIME OFF

2.1 Annual Leave

Full-time employees accrue 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, credited monthly
at 1.5 days per month. New employees begin accruing leave from their first day of employment.
Unused leave may be carried forward to the following year, subject to a maximum carryover of
10 days. Leave above the carryover limit is forfeited at year end unless extenuating
circumstances are approved by HR.

Annual leave requests must be submitted at least one week in advance for durations of 1-3 days,
and at least four weeks in advance for leave of 4 days or more. Approval is subject to business
needs and manager discretion.

2.2 Federal Holidays

Nova Software observes the 10 federally recognized public holidays in the United States. These
holidays are provided in addition to annual leave and do not count against the 18-day annual
leave accrual. A list of observed holidays for the upcoming year is published on the internal
HR portal each November.

2.3 Sick Leave

Employees are entitled to up to 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. Sick leave does
not carry over between calendar years and is not paid out upon separation. For absences
exceeding three consecutive days, employees may be required to provide documentation from a
licensed healthcare provider. Sick leave may be used to care for an immediate family member
under the same conditions.

2.4 Bereavement Leave

Employees may take up to 5 days of paid bereavement leave following the death of an immediate
family member (spouse, child, or parent), and up to 2 days for extended family members
(siblings, grandparents, or in-laws). Employees needing additional time may use accrued annual
leave or request unpaid leave.

SECTION 3: REMOTE WORK POLICY

3.1 Eligibility

Remote work is available to employees who have completed their 6-month probationary period
and whose role does not require regular on-site presence. Eligibility is determined by the
employee's manager in consultation with HR. Certain client-facing roles may have additional
on-site requirements.

3.2 Remote Work Schedule

Eligible employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. At least 2 days per week must be
worked from the designated Nova Software office or an approved co-working location. Remote days
may not be accumulated or carried forward to future weeks. Employees on performance improvement
plans may have remote work privileges temporarily suspended at the manager's discretion.

3.3 Home Office Requirements

Employees working remotely are responsible for maintaining a suitable workspace with reliable
internet connectivity (minimum 25 Mbps download speed) and a quiet environment appropriate
for video calls. Nova Software provides a home office stipend to support these requirements
(see Section 5.2). Employees must ensure their workspace meets all applicable ergonomic
guidelines published on the internal health and safety portal.

3.4 Data Security While Remote

Employees must use the company VPN when accessing internal systems from outside the office
network. Personal devices must not be used to store company data. Any security incident must
be reported to the IT department within 24 hours. Failure to comply with data security
requirements while working remotely may result in loss of remote work privileges.

SECTION 4: PERFORMANCE REVIEWS

4.1 Review Cycle

Performance reviews are conducted twice per year: in February and August. The February review
covers the second half of the prior calendar year, and the August review covers the first half
of the current year. Managers are responsible for scheduling reviews within the designated
review window (January 15 through February 28 for the winter cycle; July 15 through August 31
for the summer cycle). Missing a review window requires HR approval to reschedule.

4.2 Rating Scale

Performance is assessed on a four-point scale:
- Exceeds Expectations: Consistently surpasses goals and demonstrates exemplary impact
- Meets Expectations: Fully achieves goals and meets the standard for the role
- Needs Improvement: Partially meets goals; a development plan is required
- Unsatisfactory: Fails to meet core expectations; immediate action is required

4.3 Performance Improvement Plans

Employees who receive an Unsatisfactory rating in two consecutive review cycles may be placed
on a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP). The PIP outlines specific, measurable objectives and
a 90-day timeline for improvement. Employees must be given at least 30 days written notice
before a PIP begins. A manager may also initiate a PIP outside the standard review cycle if
performance concerns are severe enough to warrant immediate action.

4.4 Merit Increases

Merit-based salary increases are determined following the August review cycle and take effect
on October 1. Increases are based on performance rating, market data, and budget availability.
There is no guaranteed minimum increase. Employees rated Exceeds Expectations are eligible for
increases of up to 10% of their base salary. Employees rated Needs Improvement or Unsatisfactory
are not eligible for merit increases in that cycle.

SECTION 5: EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT

5.1 Business Travel

Nova Software reimburses reasonable and necessary business travel expenses. Airfare must be
booked in economy class for flights under 6 hours; business class may be approved for flights
exceeding 6 hours with prior manager authorization. Hotel accommodations are reimbursable up
to $200 per night in standard markets and $350 per night in high-cost cities including New York,
San Francisco, London, and Tokyo. Meal expenses during business travel are reimbursed up to
$50 per day, inclusive of gratuity but exclusive of alcohol.

5.2 Home Office Stipend

Employees who work remotely at least 1 day per week are eligible for a home office stipend of
$75 per month. This stipend is added directly to the employee's paycheck and is intended to
offset costs for internet service, utilities, and office supplies. The stipend is considered
taxable income and will appear on the employee's W-2 form at year end.

5.3 Client Entertainment

Client entertainment expenses above $100 require advance written approval from the employee's
manager. Receipts must be submitted within 30 days of the expense. Reimbursement requests
submitted after 60 days will not be honored under any circumstances. Expenses for entertainment
not directly related to business purposes are not reimbursable.

SECTION 6: EQUIPMENT POLICY

6.1 Laptop Stipend

Nova Software provides a laptop procurement stipend of $1,500 every 3 years. Employees may
use this stipend to purchase a laptop through the company's approved vendor portal. All laptops
purchased with the stipend remain company property. Employees wishing to keep their laptop upon
departure may purchase it at its current depreciated book value, as determined by the finance
department.

6.2 Peripheral Equipment

In addition to the laptop stipend, employees receive an annual peripheral equipment budget of
$300 per year. This budget covers monitors, keyboards, mice, webcams, headsets, and ergonomic
accessories such as standing desk converters. Unused portions of the peripheral budget do not
carry over between calendar years. Peripheral purchases are also company property unless the
employee elects to purchase them at depreciated value upon departure.

6.3 Equipment Return

All company equipment must be returned within 5 business days of an employee's last working
day. Equipment not returned in good working condition, ordinary wear excepted, will result in
the replacement cost being deducted from the employee's final paycheck in accordance with
applicable state law.

SECTION 7: EMPLOYEE BENEFITS

7.1 Health Insurance

Nova Software covers 80% of the monthly premium for the standard health insurance plan for
the employee. Dependent coverage is available; Nova Software covers 50% of the additional
premium for dependent enrollment. Employees may enroll in or modify health coverage during the
annual open enrollment period in November, or following a qualifying life event such as marriage,
birth, adoption, or loss of other coverage.

7.2 Dental and Vision

Nova Software covers 60% of the monthly premium for dental and vision coverage. These plans
are offered through the same open enrollment process as health insurance. Orthodontia coverage
is included in the dental plan with a lifetime maximum benefit of $2,000.

7.3 Retirement Plan

Nova Software offers a 401(k) retirement savings plan administered through Vanguard. The
company matches employee contributions dollar-for-dollar up to 4% of the employee's annual
base salary. Employees are fully vested in company match contributions after 2 years of service.
Contributions may begin on the first payroll cycle after 90 days of employment.

SECTION 8: PARENTAL LEAVE

8.1 Paid Parental Leave

Nova Software provides 12 weeks of fully paid parental leave to all eligible employees
following the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child. Leave must be initiated within
12 months of the qualifying event. Both primary and secondary caregivers are eligible for the
same 12-week benefit. Employees must provide at least 30 days notice of planned parental leave
whenever practicable.

8.2 Additional Unpaid Leave

Employees who exhaust their paid parental leave may request up to 4 additional weeks of
unpaid leave under this policy. This unpaid leave runs concurrently with any applicable
federal or state family leave entitlements. Benefits coverage continues during both the paid
and approved unpaid portions of parental leave.

SECTION 9: RESIGNATION AND SEPARATION

9.1 Resignation Notice

Employees with less than 2 years of service are expected to provide a minimum of 2 weeks
written notice of resignation. Employees with 2 or more years of service are expected to
provide a minimum of 4 weeks written notice. Nova Software appreciates longer notice periods
when operationally feasible and will accommodate transition requests where possible.

9.2 Severance

In the event of involuntary separation other than termination for cause, employees are eligible
for severance equal to 1 week of base salary per completed year of service, up to a maximum of
8 weeks. Severance payment is contingent on the employee signing a separation and release
agreement within 21 days of receiving the agreement.

9.3 Final Paycheck

Final paychecks, including any accrued but unused annual leave, will be issued on the next
regular payroll date following the employee's last working day, or earlier as required by
applicable state law.
